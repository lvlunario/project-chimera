## 3–8 minutes — concept: separate execution, evidence and acceptance

Chimera treats these as different authorities:

| Layer | What it may do | What it must not claim |
|---|---|---|
| `verify-link` / acceptance-preparation command | validate input, execute the bounded workflow and publish new evidence | human usability or phase approval |
| durable evidence, reports and handoff | preserve exact inputs, results, identities and trace links | producer identity, trusted time or physical-radio authenticity |
| local read-only dashboard | cross-validate sealed artifacts, show status and download the same handoff | change a run, repair evidence or choose between conflicting identities |
| operator/PM | judge usefulness, usability, limits and phase disposition | that an unperformed check passed |

Example: sample 2 is 2.0 dB against a 3.0 dB threshold. The CLI records a valid engineering
FAIL; the report and handoff trace that result; the dashboard refuses to recalculate or edit
it. A human still decides whether the workflow communicates the failure well enough to use.

Teaching note for Leo: this is like separating a test conductor, a controlled test record
and the customer who accepts the result. Letting the display rewrite the record would make it
both witness and editor, weakening the audit trail.
