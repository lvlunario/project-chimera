## Limits and risks

- Synthetic faults test detection and traceability, not RF physics or hardware behavior.
- SHA-256 proves content identity, not authorship, trusted time or source authenticity.
- The dashboard is local, loopback-only and read-only; it has no accounts, TLS or production
  deployment approval.
- A `running` task after interruption remains outcome-unknown and is never auto-retried.
- Passing automation is engineering evidence and cannot substitute for Leo's actual response
  or final acceptance.
