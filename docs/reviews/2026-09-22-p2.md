## Known limits that remain after P2 approval

- Trusted in-process callbacks are not sandboxed and may hang or affect the host.
- A `running` task may already have produced an external effect; default recovery stops.
- No exactly-once, power-loss, defective-disk, network-filesystem or live-hardware claim.
- Operation IDs, local rows and source hashes identify content; they are not signatures or
  proof of an authorized producer.
- Journal and immutable evidence databases are separate; their handoff is validated but not
  one atomic transaction.
- Linux local-file lifetime ownership is the tested recovery environment.

These limits are explicit prototype boundaries. A requested change to them may require an
October 17 reforecast rather than consuming final stabilization time.
